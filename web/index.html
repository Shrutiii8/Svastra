ENGINEERING & SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

How Does Svastra Work?

Most heat safety protocols rely exclusively on ambient thermometers. Svastra bridges the physiological gap by pairing wearable biometric telemetry with hyper-local meteorology and individual baselines.

01

Wearable Telemetry Sync

Outdoor workers wear affordable commercial smartwatches or fitness bands from any manufacturer. The Svastra Mobile App taps into native Android Health Connect to securely retrieve optical heart rate, step count, and SpO₂ readings without requiring continuous paired Bluetooth connections.

  • Universal WearOS & Smartwatch integration
  • Batch idempotent telemetry ingestion
  • Encrypted storage on-device
02

Hyper-Local Microclimate

The backend connects asynchronously with Open-Meteo atmospheric APIs, capturing ambient temperature, relative humidity, direct solar exposure, and wind speed for the job site coordinates. An intelligent 5-minute cache with simulated fallback guarantees zero downtime.

  • Apparent Heat Index & Wet-Bulb approximation
  • Automatic cached fallback mechanisms
  • Hyper-local job site granularity
03

Personal Baseline Modeling

A heart rate of 115 bpm might be normal for a 22-year-old carrying rebar, but dangerous for a 55-year-old vendor. Svastra rejects one-size-fits-all limits. It builds a rolling 7-day personalized baseline ($HR_0$) for every worker, assessing biometric changes relative to their own normal biology.

  • Rolling 7-day baseline normalization
  • Individual differential calculation ($HR - HR_{baseline}$)
  • Categorized by occupational workload
04

Deterministic Triage Engine

Instead of an unpredictable black-box neural net, Svastra utilizes an audited clinical rule-based engine. It triangulates heart rate elevation, ambient temperature thresholds, and data recency into instant triage statuses with actionable safety actions.

  • Instant 3-tier status: Normal, Monitor, Review
  • Environmental escalation rules ($T \ge 40^\circ\text{C}$)
  • Zero hallucinations & full auditability

CAPABILITIES & BENEFITS

Built for Real-World Occupational Safety

Engineered from ground up to survive harsh Indian summer job sites, unreliable connectivity, and heterogeneous hardware.

🧬

Personalized Biometric Baselines

Accounts for natural biological variance, age, and individual cardiovascular fitness by calculating rolling 7-day personalized reference envelopes rather than rigid generic thresholds.

⌚

Universal Wearables Sync

Integrated with Android Health Connect. Works natively with Samsung Galaxy Watches, Pixel Watches, WearOS devices, and commercial fitness bands without proprietary vendor lock-in.

🌡️

Microclimate Intelligence

Retrieves live wet-bulb and dry-bulb ambient conditions via Open-Meteo. Automatically correlates temperature spikes with worker heart rate strain indices.

🚨

Supervisor Multi-Worker Triage

Site managers can view hundreds of outdoor workers at a single glance, filtered by category (Construction, Delivery, Vendor, Police) and sorted by risk severity.

🔒

Privacy-By-Design & Consent

Requires explicit worker opt-in before collecting vitals. No GPS tracking or background tracking cookies are collected, ensuring worker trust and ethical compliance.

⚡

Offline Resilient & Standalone APK

Compiles to a standalone 104MB Android APK with complete offline asset bundling and local SQLite cache when working in remote cell-dead zones.
